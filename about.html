THE SHORT VERSION

Hello, I'm Naina.

I'm a senior at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, double majoring in neuroscience and data science with a minor in chemistry.

Through clinical experiences, research, and data science, I've become interested in how evidence and communication can work together to improve healthcare. I'm especially drawn to working with children and families.

Outside of science, I enjoy photography, poetry, design, and documentary storytelling. I like projects that make me pay attention to the experiences and perspectives of other people.

After graduation, I hope to keep learning through research and clinical work as I prepare for a career in medicine.
